Beary style guide

One set of rules for color, type, spacing, buttons and cards, so every screen looks like it belongs to the same app. Everything shown here uses the live values from the prototype.

Principles

Four rules from the design review. When a screen feels off, it usually breaks one of these.

1Whitespace belongs between sections. Do not trap it inside oversized cards.

2Cards fit their content. No fixed heights, no empty space to fill.

3One clear primary action. Secondary actions look quieter, so the eye lands on the main one first.

4No duplicate actions on one screen. If two buttons go to the same place, keep one.

Color

A dark indigo background with glass cards that step from lighter to darker blue down the page. Lavender is reserved for the main action.

✓Nothing is darker than the page background.

✓Mood and energy colors always match the four map areas, everywhere they appear.

×No green as an interface accent. Green appears only inside data (a quadrant or a series).

×No red or warning styling for ordinary moods or logged experiences.

Type

Playfair Display for titles and headings, Figtree for everything people read or tap. Six sizes, no others.

✓16px is the smallest size for body text people need to read.

✓Put 16px between a heading and the text below it.

×Do not invent in-between sizes. Pick the nearest step.

Spacing

A short scale on a 4px grid. Screens use only these values.

✓Between sections, use the 32px. Between cards, 24px. Never squeeze them together.

✓Every tap target is at least 44px tall.

Buttons

Two weights of button, so the main action is obvious.

✓Primary is lavender. Use it once per card for the action most people want.

✓Secondary is blue glass. Use it for going to another screen or for optional actions, and add an arrow when it opens another screen.

✓Chips choose between options. The selected chip turns lavender.

×Do not stack three buttons of the same weight. Make one primary, the rest secondary.

×Do not repeat an action that already appears on the screen.

Cards

Cards are glass: a soft gradient, a thin light border and a blur. Each card down a page is one step darker than the last, within the blue range.

✓Radius 24, padding 24, 24 between cards.

✓Home shows a plain-language observation. Charts live in Patterns.

×No cards inside cards unless the inner one is a control.

Data and charts

People should read what was recorded, never feel judged by it.

✓Label every count with its unit: "4 of 6 check-ins", not "4 of 6".

✓Color a day or dot by its map area. Color shows where entries fell, not whether a day was good or bad.

✓Keep a dashed circle for "nothing recorded" and a legend under every chart.

×No predictions, no warnings, no claims about cause.

Before a screen ships

Run through this list for every new or changed screen.

  • Screen sides are 24px, sections 32px apart, cards 24px apart.
  • Only the six type sizes are used, and headings sit 16px above their text.
  • There is one primary button, and no action appears twice.
  • Every tap target is at least 44px tall, with a visible focus ring.
  • Colors come from the tokens, nothing is darker than the background, and meaning never depends on color alone.
  • Counts show their unit, and charts have a legend.
  • Words are calm, American spelling, and describe records rather than predict.
